The session

How it works

A turn, out loud

1

F5, say what you want

The microphone is closed until you press F5. It records until you pause, or until a second F5. The speech is transcribed on your Mac and typed into the agent as the prompt.

2

narrate, show, then edit

The agent opens with one sentence on what it understood, then says what it will change and why; Neovim jumps to the lines. After the edit, the change is drawn inside the file, added lines highlighted, removed lines in place.

3

ask, then wait for F5

When a decision is ambiguous, the agent asks aloud and waits. Press F5 and answer.

F6 cuts the agent mid-sentence and opens the microphone for you. F7 shows the agent's own pane, otherwise hidden; it appears by itself when the agent waits for a permission or a trust dialog. F8, pressed twice, ends the session. Hand edits between turns are diffed and passed to the agent at its next prompt, so it never overwrites them unknowingly.

What runs where

Runs on your Mac

  • Listening is whisper.cpp, speaking is macOS say or Kokoro, recording is sox. All on your Mac; the whisper model is 1.6 GB and Apple Silicon is recommended.
  • The microphone opens only when you press F5, and closes when you pause or press it again.
  • The agent's own conversation still goes to its provider, Claude Code to Anthropic and Codex to OpenAI, as it does without pair. pair sends nothing anywhere. Turn Wi-Fi off and press F5: the transcript still arrives; only the agent's reply waits for the network.
  • Downloads are pinned and SHA-256 verified.
  • Your repository is left as it is. What pair keeps for a session, the scratchpad included, lives in .pair/, which ignores itself in git.
  • The source is private for now. The releases, their checksums and the Homebrew cask are public.

Claude Code and Codex

Agents

Pick one

Claude Code by default; pair init --agent codex makes Codex CLI the default, pair --agent codex picks it for one session. A session remembers its agent.

How it connects

When pair starts the agent, it hands it hooks (before and after edits, at prompts, at the end of a turn, at permission prompts), three MCP tools, narrate, show and ask, and the rules of pairing, for that process only. Nothing is written into the repository, and an agent you open without pair knows nothing of it. No fork of the agent, no daemon, no cloud.

Sessions

Each session is its own. It can be named, listed, continued with the agent's conversation resumed, read back, or replayed with the narrations spoken again.

Questions

FAQ

Why macOS only?

It speaks through macOS say and gets the microphone through macOS's own permission prompt; nothing in it is portable yet. macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon recommended. No Windows or Linux build.

Do I need to configure Neovim?

No. Without a config of your own, pair brings a theme, the tree and the keys; with yours, it changes nothing. Neovim 0.10 or later.

Does my voice leave the Mac?

No. sox records and whisper.cpp transcribes, on your Mac. Only the transcript reaches the agent, as text. pair sends nothing anywhere.

Which agents and models?

Claude Code, the default, and Codex CLI, with whatever model each uses. Voices are macOS say or Kokoro (54 voices, English and Brazilian Portuguese among them); listening is whisper.cpp large-v3-turbo.

What does it cost?

Nothing. pair is free and the release is MIT-licensed. The agent's own plan is separate, as it is without pair.
